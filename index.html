Quick-check aid. Curves were read from scanned RFM charts and may differ slightly from a hand plot, most near the chart edges. The approved RFM charts remain the reference: if this app and the chart disagree, use the chart. Observe all engine, transmission and aircraft limits during the check.

Method: Corrected TQ from Fig 4-5 by interpolating between the altitude lines. MAX ITT from Fig 4-10 (−1000 to below 2000 ft) or Fig 4-11 (2000 to 5000 ft), interpolating between the OAT curves. At exactly 2000 ft both charts apply and the lower (more conservative) MAX ITT is used. PAC margin = MAX ITT − engine ITT; acceptable when greater than 0.
